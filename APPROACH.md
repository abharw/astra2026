A sequential log of Arav's AR work, Akeil's VR work, and their collaboration with Astra. Each section records human direction, model/tool work, corrections, and observed results. AR technical decisions live in [architecture.md](docs/architecture.md), with sources in [RESEARCH.md](docs/research.md). The VR account is drawn from Akeil's [build journal](app/VR/docs/BUILD_JOURNAL.md), [live testing record](app/VR/docs/QUEST_LIVE_TESTING.md), and [rack production history](app/VR/datacenter-rack/docs/HACKATHON_PROCESS.md).
