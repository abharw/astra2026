### Correction produced by rewatch

The initial question to the user mistakenly put the kitchen on the left of dining. Rewatching 47–57.79 s at 2 fps caught the error: **48.535 s shows dining windows on the left and kitchen on the right within the same frame**. At 57.000 s the reverse view shows the kitchen reflected in the fireplace mirror, supporting the opposite-side relationship. The final map uses the corrected right-side layout. Human confirmation has been requested and is still pending.

### How I would complete the missing 3D space

This is a proposal, not a generated or validated 3D reconstruction:

- Start with a long circulation axis and separate room volumes attached at the observed openings. Use the observed furniture/wall anchors to orient each room.
- Model a local hall jog and stair void rather than flattening them into one rectangular room. Leave the lower level empty and labeled unknown.
- Add the sitting-room and dining-room bays as adjustable polygons; their exact angles and depths are assumptions.
- Use simple, adjustable bathroom and bedroom boxes. Hidden walls should retain an inferred label rather than receive invented doors or furniture.
- Keep two versions of the study connection: a second hall entrance into the same study, or an unidentified additional room. The video alone does not settle this.
- Use arbitrary model units until at least one real length is supplied. Recovering true scale, wall thickness, ceiling height or exact camera poses was not attempted here.
